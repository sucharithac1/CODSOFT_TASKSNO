## Dataset
- Source: cleaned_data.csv
- Columns: Quantity, Price, Category, City, Payment_Method, Order_Date
- Added calculated column: Sales = Quantity × Price

## Visualizations
1. Sales by Category → Bar chart
2. Sales by City → Bar chart
3. Payment Method Distribution → Pie chart
4. Monthly Sales Trend → Line chart

## Insights
- Electronics category generated the highest sales.
- Delhi contributed the most revenue among cities.
- Debit Card was the most popular payment method.
- Sales peaked in March and May, showing seasonal demand.
